Intent & Outcome

Before this change, the only way to view a cycle's verdict and cost was the full markdown trail (dozens of lines). After this change, `claude-trail <id> --compact` emits exactly 3 lines — title, verdict, cost — enabling fast terminal-glance checks. The flag is mutually exclusive with --format json, --out, and --since, each rejected with a clear error message.
